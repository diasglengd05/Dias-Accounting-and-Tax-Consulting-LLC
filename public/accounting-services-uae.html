Frequently Asked Questions

Key statutory facts on accounting standards, software, and bookkeeping rules in the UAE.

Are businesses in the UAE required to maintain books of accounts?
Yes, under UAE Commercial Companies Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2021) and Corporate Tax Law, all UAE commercial entities must maintain proper accounting records and financial statements for at least five to seven years.
Which accounting software do you support for UAE businesses?
We are certified advisors and support leading cloud accounting platforms including Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, and Xero, tailored to UAE FTA VAT and Corporate Tax compliance.
Can you assist with backlog accounting for overdue tax filings?
Yes, our backlog accounting team specializes in reconstructing incomplete financial records, reconciling missing invoices and bank statements, and producing audited financial statements for late corporate tax and VAT filings.
