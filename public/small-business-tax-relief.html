Small Business Corporate Tax Relief in Dubai

The UAE has extended the Small Business Relief (SBR) deadline to December 31, 2029. Eligible Resident Persons with revenue of AED 3 million or less can be treated as having no taxable income.

Are You Eligible?

  • Revenue must be AED 3 million or less in the current and previous tax periods.
  • Qualifying Free Zone Persons (QFZPs) are excluded from this relief.
