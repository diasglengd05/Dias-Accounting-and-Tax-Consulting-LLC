Frequently Asked Questions

Essential insights into UAE Corporate Tax laws, VAT rules, and FTA audit requirements.

Who needs to register for Corporate Tax in Dubai?
All businesses operating in the UAE, including mainland companies and Free Zone entities, must register for Corporate Tax with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA), regardless of their turnover or profit levels.
What is the VAT registration threshold in the UAE?
Mandatory VAT registration is required for businesses whose taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 in the previous 12 months. Voluntary registration is available for businesses exceeding AED 187,500.
How does Dias Accounting help prevent FTA tax penalties?
Our certified tax agents ensure all deadlines for Corporate Tax registration, quarterly VAT return filings, and Transfer Pricing documentation are strictly met, conducting pre-filing reconciliations to prevent administrative penalties under Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023.
