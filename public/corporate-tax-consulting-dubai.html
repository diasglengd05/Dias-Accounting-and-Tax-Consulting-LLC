Are You Ready for the UAE Corporate Tax Deadlines?

Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 introduces strict statutory obligations for all UAE legal entities. Missing critical deadlines or misinterpreting rules carries severe financial and operational repercussions.

Compliance Risks, Registration Deadlines & Penalty Exposures

Under UAE Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023, failure to submit a Corporate Tax registration application within the timelines established by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) incurs an immediate administrative penalty of AED 10,000. Many business owners mistakenly believe that only profitable companies or those exceeding the AED 375,000 threshold must take action.

In reality, registration is mandatory for all UAE mainland companies and Free Zone entities regardless of turnover or profit levels. Navigating the complexity surrounding the AED 375,000 threshold (where 0% applies to taxable profits up to AED 375,000 and 9% applies above) and qualifying Free Zone exemptions requires meticulous bookkeeping, transaction classification, and timely filings.

⚠️ Late Registration Penalties

FTA enforces an immediate AED 10,000 fine for entities missing the statutory registration cut-off based on license issuance dates.

⚖️ Threshold Misconceptions

The AED 375,000 threshold determines tax calculation, not registration exemptions. Full statutory returns must still be filed every financial year.

🏛️ Free Zone Disqualification

Breaching the strict 5% or AED 5,000,000 de minimis threshold for non-qualifying revenue causes immediate loss of 0% QFZP status for 5 consecutive years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clarifications on the UAE 9% Corporate Tax regime, registration deadlines, and Free Zone preferential rates.

Do Free Zone businesses need to register for UAE Corporate Tax even if they qualify for 0%?
Yes, registration is mandatory regardless of revenue or tax rate. Every Free Zone entity must obtain a Corporate Tax TRN from the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) and submit an annual corporate tax return to maintain compliance and substantiate its Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) status.
What is the corporate tax rate in the UAE?
The UAE corporate tax rate is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000, and 9% on taxable income exceeding AED 375,000. Additionally, Qualifying Free Zone Persons (QFZPs) that satisfy economic substance and de minimis requirements may benefit from a 0% preferential rate on Qualifying Income.
